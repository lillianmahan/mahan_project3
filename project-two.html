Double Exposure - Black and White

Using everyday pictures to create a compelling double exposure using black and white film.

Overview

This project was designed to explore the creative possibilities of double exposure techniques using black and white film.

The Challenge

The challenge was to create a visually striking double exposure image using only black and white film, while maintaining the integrity of both subjects.

Role & Tools

  • Photographer and Creator
  • Black and White Film, Darkroom Equipment

The Solution

Results

The project successfully demonstrated the creative potential of double exposure techniques in black and white photography.

Reflection

This project taught me the importance of careful planning and execution in achieving desired visual outcomes.
